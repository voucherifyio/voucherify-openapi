In this tutorial, you create one discount coupon. The coupon sets shipping to 0 and sets every other item to 95% of its unit price.

The discount is a **Product discount** of type **New price**. One discount can set a different new price on the **Shipping** product and on a product collection. The **Free shipping** category is a separate discount, and its value cannot be a formula. Read [Discount types and effects](/build/discount-types-and-effects) for the other discount categories.

## What you will have

A generic code, `SHIP5`. In the Demo Shop, **Shipping** in the order summary is 0, and each other unit costs 95% of its price. A unit priced at 20.00 costs 19.00.

## Before you start

Your checkout sends products and shipping as separate order items. The shipping item is the built-in **Shipping** product. The **Product discount** picker lists that product.

The 5% applies to a collection of every product whose name is not Shipping. **All Products** includes **Shipping**, so this tutorial uses a new collection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open product collections">
    Go to **Campaign hub** > **Product collections** > **Create collection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the collection">
    Name the collection `Products except shipping`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exclude Shipping">
    Add a filter on **Name**. Set the condition to **is not**. Set the value to `Shipping`.

    `Shipping` is the built-in product name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the collection">
    Click **Save as collection** and choose **Dynamic collection**.

    The collection updates when products are added. An SKU is included only when the SKU itself matches the filter. See [SKUs and dynamic collections](/prepare/product-collections#skus-and-dynamic-collections).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create the discount coupon

First, create a campaign for the discount coupon.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign builder">
    Go to **Campaign hub** > **Campaigns** > **Create campaign** and select **Discount coupons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the code">
    Name the campaign `Free shipping and 5% off`.

    Select **Generic code** and enter `SHIP5`.

    For bulk codes, time frame, and the other campaign settings, see [Create discount coupons](/build/create-discount-coupons).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the time frame">
    Open **Time frame** and leave the settings as they are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the discount builder">
    Go to **Discount value**. Click **Add discount** > **Product discount**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the shipping price and the item price

The discount builder opens on **Discount**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose New price">
    In **Discount type**, select **New price: Apply the discount to item subtotal**.

    Under **Discounted products**, leave **Selected**. **Each** would include **Shipping** in the 5% price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set shipping to 0">
    On the first row, open **Select product or collection**. Choose **Products** and select **Shipping**.

    Set **New price** to `0`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the 5% price">
    Click **Add another**.

    On the new row, open **Select product or collection**. Choose **Collections** and select `Products except shipping`.

    On **New price**, click **Build formula** and enter:

    ```text
    ORDER_ITEM_PRICE * 0.95
    ```

    **order item price** is the unit price. The new price is applied to each unit. When the builder shows **Formula is valid**, save the formula.

    Set the **Fallback for New price** to a desired value.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Leave **Conditions** empty and click **Save**.

    To limit the coupon by order value, customer segment, or channel, add a validation rule on **Conditions**. See [Create validation rules](/optimize/create-validation-rules).

    Leave **Metadata** as they are. On the campaign summary, click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the discount

Go to **Marketer hub** > **Demo shop**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a product">
    Add one unit of a product to the cart. The order summary includes a **Shipping** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the code">
    In **Discount code**, enter `SHIP5` and click **Validate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    **Shipping** is 0. The product unit costs 95% of its price. A unit priced at 20.00 costs 19.00. Two units cost 38.00.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Validation rules">

Validation rules decide whether `SHIP5` can be redeemed. The new prices decide what the customer pays.

This coupon is a product discount, so Voucherify creates a validation rule for **Shipping** and `Products except shipping`. You can edit that rule and add conditions. You cannot remove it.

You can limit the coupon with:

- **Audience**, such as a customer segment or customer metadata
- **Prices and quantities**, such as order value or the number of items
- **Budget constraints**, such as redemptions per customer or per period
- **Metadata** on the order or the redemption

See [Create validation rules](/optimize/create-validation-rules) and [Validation rule reference](/optimize/validation-rules-reference).
