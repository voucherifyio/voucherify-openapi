## Default retention periods

The default retention periods are as follows.

| Category | What it covers | Default retention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Redemptions | Confirmed promo code, gift card, and promotion tier usages, including failed attempts and redemption rollback records | Indefinitely |
| Validations | Real-time discount eligibility checks | 30 days |
| Order line items | Detailed item-level basket structures | 2 years |
| Order discounts | Order- and item-level discount mappings | 2 years |
| Voucher transactions | Points adjustments and gift card credit transaction records | 2 years |

## Related limits

Redemption rollback has a 3-month action window: you can create a rollback only for a successful redemption that is at most 3 months old. That limit does not delete data.

Once a rollback succeeds, the rollback record is stored with redemptions and is kept indefinitely, the same as the original redemption. See [Validations and redemptions](/optimize/validations-and-redemptions) and [Fraud prevention best practices](/optimize/fraud-prevention-best-practices).
